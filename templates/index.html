12-Band Multispectral Pipeline

AquaSeg accepts 128×128 GeoTIFF tiles containing 12 co-registered spectral bands and topographic elevation. The neural model synthesizes surface reflectance and physical hydrological indices:

Channel Band Name Central Wavelength Hydrological Purpose
Ch 1B01 (Coastal Aerosol)443 nmAtmospheric correction & coastal margin
Ch 2B02 (Blue)490 nmTrue Color Composite (B) & deep water
Ch 3B03 (Green)560 nmTrue Color (G) & MNDWI numerator
Ch 4B04 (Red)665 nmTrue Color (R) & suspended sediment
Ch 5B05 (Red Edge 1)705 nmAquatic vegetation boundary
Ch 6B06 (Red Edge 2)740 nmCanopy reflectance thresholding
Ch 7B07 (Red Edge 3)783 nmVegetation plateau detection
Ch 8B08 (Broad NIR)842 nmHigh water absorption (NDWI denominator)
Ch 9B8A (Narrow NIR)865 nmWater/vegetation discrimination
Ch 10B09 (Water Vapour)945 nmAtmospheric column moisture
Ch 11B11 (SWIR 1)1610 nmMNDWI absorption (Zero in water)
Ch 12DEM (Elevation)Elevation (m)Eliminates mountain shadow false positives
MNDWI (Modified NDWI) (B03 - B11) / (B03 + B11 + ε) Suppresses built-up and soil noise, highlights open water.
AWEI_sh (Automated Shadow Index) B02 + 2.5·B03 - 1.5·(B08 + B11) - 0.25·B12 Removes shadow confusion in mountainous and urban terrain.
